01 // How ReviewPulse Works For Your Business

Step 1: Verify Your Google Review Link

Sign in, enter your business name, city, and official Google Business review URL (g.page/r/...). Our built-in link authenticator verifies your listing so every customer lands on the right page.

Step 2: Print Your Counter QR Standee

Download your branded high-res PNG poster with your logo and place it at your billing desk. Happy 4–5★ customers get a quick review draft & open Google Maps; 1–3★ complaints go privately to your inbox.

Step 3: Send 1-Click WhatsApp Nudges

Enter a customer's name and number right after billing to send a warm review invite in English, Tamil, Tanglish, or Hindi, plus track Day-0 and Day-3 follow-ups.

Step 4: Auto-Reply in 21+ Languages

Paste any review or sync your Google Business Profile to generate 3 natural, local-SEO replies in the reviewer's exact language, with built-in spam detection & clinic privacy protection.
